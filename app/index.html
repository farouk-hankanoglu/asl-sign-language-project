ASL ALPHABET REFERENCE — HOW TO SIGN EACH LETTER

Tap chart to enlarge · Gallaudet ASL alphabet chart, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons

Note on J and Z: these two letters are dynamic in real ASL — they involve drawing a motion in the air (J traces a hook, Z traces a zigzag). This system recognises static handshapes only, so J and Z are approximated by their starting handshape and are the least reliable letters. This is a known limitation.
